Your keys. Now with a voice.

Custom Voice: select Custom Voice in the Mac app. Recording 1 plays on other keys; recording 2 on physical A–Z and 0–9 keys. New presses restart the voice, so fast typing cuts off the previous clip. Fn/media keys play only when macOS delivers a keyboard event. The two recordings are included in the public download. Sound Library → Check now fetches available online packs. Updates keep your selected sound and volume; app feature updates still require a newer app version.

Choose a sound to preview. This website does not monitor your keyboard.

PICK IT. KEEP IT.

Your sound today.
Your sound tomorrow.

Your sound style, volume, key-hold preference and paused state are saved on your Mac. New in v1.5: future sound packs download automatically and appear in the menu without reinstalling the app.

Turn on Launch at login to keep your settings after restarting. Change the sound whenever you like, or pause for some quiet.

Sounds resume after you log in and the app starts. A paused app stays paused, even after a restart.

GET STARTED

Three steps to a different typing mood.

01 / INSTALL

Move it to Applications

Unzip the download and move Rainy Desk English to Applications before opening it. Keep it there and run only one copy or language edition at a time.

02 / ALLOW

Let your keyboard play

Click Grant access in the app. Enable Rainy Desk English under Input Monitoring. If it is missing, use + to add the installed app, then quit and reopen it.

03 / KEEP

Choose it. Keep it.

Install v1.5 once to enable future sound downloads. Pick a sound, adjust the volume and enable Launch at login. You can mute selected apps or turn on sounds while holding a key.

On your Mac. Stays on your Mac.

Sound playback works offline with no account. The app checks the public sound library at launch and every 6 hours; automatic downloads can be switched off. It does not save or upload typed text. Keyboard access is used only to trigger sounds.

A note about this beta

Mac only; Windows is not supported. Secure input and some password fields may stay silent. The Apple Silicon build is not yet tested on physical hardware.

macOS says Apple could not verify the app.

Click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll to Security and, if you trust this download, click Open Anyway next to Rainy Desk English. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. Do not disable system security protections.

The app opens, but typing is silent.

Open Anyway only allows the app to launch. You must also enable Input Monitoring for the installed English app. Try Preview sounds first. If preview works, check Enable keyboard sounds and permission status. After replacing an app, you may need to remove its old permission entry, add the installed copy again, and restart it.
